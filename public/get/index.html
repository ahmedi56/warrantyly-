What it does

Receipts that read themselves

Take a photo and the product, store, date and price are filled in. Check them, then save.

A reminder before it’s too late

Warrantyly warns you ahead of the end date, then again closer to it, at the time of day you choose.

Ready when something breaks

Try the quick fixes first. If it still fails, share a ready-made claim summary with the store.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android. Your phone saves a file called Warrantyly.apk.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps this one time.
  3. Open Warrantyly and tap Get started.

Android shows a warning for apps that don’t come from Google Play. This is an early version that is shared directly. On iPhone or a computer, open the browser version. Reminders only work in the Android app.
